# What each decision file governs

Every human and authoritative ruling in this project is a row of readable text in this folder.
The analyst's rulings carry who decided and when, and a reason wherever a proposal was rejected;
the official record's rulings carry their source. The build reads these files; it never writes
a ruling of its own. Row counts are as of 2026-08-20 and can grow with a later delivery.

## The operative decisions

**`stage2_decisions.csv`, 231 rows.** The answer to every retirement the official record did
not settle outright. Nineteen method columns are regenerated by the queue builder; five analyst
columns carry the decision word, who decided, when, the reason, and whether a hold was
overridden. The decision vocabulary is fixed and an empty cell is never approval.
`stage2_decisions.xlsx` is the same file as the analyst edits it; the CSV is what the build
reads. Decided by the analyst across 2026-08-11 to 2026-08-15.

**`stage2_adjudication_queue.csv`, 231 rows.** The worklist those answers respond to: per
retirement, the proposal, the runner-up, the best candidate ruled out with the reason, and the
best candidate the classification narrowing never considered. Generated; documented in
`Readme adjudication queue.txt`.

**`decisive_distinctions.csv`, 17 rows.** The written rules about meaning: who the goods are
for, how they are packed, sterile or not, and so on. Sixteen are active, fourteen that veto
and two that only flag; one row is kept as recorded non-decisive. Each row states what the
rule says, what it may do, and its tested standing against the concordance.

**`official_constraint_sets.csv`, 5 rows.** Export retirements where the record bounds the
answer to a short list without choosing. Three bound the field, one records a bound that is
not a succession, and one requires a ruling, which the workbook carries with its reasoning.

**`medicine_families.csv`, 178 rows.** The medicine-name layer used by one check: which
medicine family each commodity string belongs to, so a succession that swaps one substance
for another can be challenged.

## The CBSA layer

**`cbsa_override_table.csv`, 122 rows.** Every pair the two concordances state, classified by
what applying it does to this delivery; 120 apply, and the file records why each of the other
two does not. Rebuilt from the extracted concordances by a documented process, not hand-typed.

**`cbsa_override_exclusions.csv`, 2 rows.** The two hand-curated departures, ricin and
saxitoxin: the concordance's first hop is followed, and the 2022 landing is corrected to the
toxin line with the full argument recorded. Decided 2026-08-01 during the correction review.

**`cbsa_partial_transfers.csv`, 11 rows.** Lines the concordance maps to themselves and to
another code at once. A single-terminal model cannot carry a partial split, so these are
excluded from the override and published as a stated limitation.

**`cbsa_override_ambiguous_imports.csv`, 8 rows.** Lines where the concordance names several
successors without ranking them. The crosswalk's own routing picks the branch, the pick falls
inside the official set, and each row says exactly that.

**`five_way_split_receipts.csv`, 7 rows.** The receipts for the hardest single case, the
five-way 2017 split of 3002.10.00.90, drawn as panel e of the examples figure.

**`leftover_line_gaps.csv`, 31 rows.** Every deleted leftover line and where its goods went,
including the ten cross-chapter cases the manuscript memo covers.

## History, kept but not read by the build

**`ADJUDICATION_FINDINGS.csv`, 155 rows.** The register of every defect found in this rebuild:
what looked wrong, the error class, the root cause, the response, and the control it added.
The build does not read it; the project's discipline depends on it.

**`imports_review_worklist_assess.xlsx`.** The similarity shortlists the analyst assessed, with
a yellow fill marking a reject. It is here because two published files cite it as the provenance
of a ruling: `cbsa_override_ambiguous_imports.csv` in `resolution_detail`, and
`five_way_split_receipts.csv` in `provenance`.

**Not in this package.** The first release's decision record
(`stage2_decisions_imports.csv`, 126 rows) and the two `*_review_worklist*` queues are superseded
and were carried only for comparison: 97 of that record's links do not exist in the operative
workbook, and its rejects mostly name no successor, which is the defect class this rebuild exists
to close. Two working notes from the adjudication sessions are likewise not here. All five remain
in the project repository. Nothing in this package reads them and no published figure rests on
them; `stage2_decisions.csv` carries all 231 rulings with their decision, reason, decided_by and
decided_on.
