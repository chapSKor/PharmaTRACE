# Gate 1: every family difference from the v1 release, explained

**CONFIRMED BY THE ANALYST ON 2026-08-18, all nine events.**

Gate 1's criterion is that every difference from the v1 released CSVs is *either zero, or explained
in writing as a deliberate improvement with the reason*. This is that writing, and every event in
it has been reviewed and accepted.

## What the differences are

`gate1/family_differences_imports.csv` holds 20 rows and `_exports.csv` holds 4. Those 24 rows are
not 24 independent differences. A family that v2 splits usually also appears in a row that v2
merges, because the same records are involved in both. Reading them as connected components over
shared membership gives **nine events, seven in imports and two in exports**.

Every one of the nine is **count-preserving**: N v1 families become N v2 families. Nothing was
created and nothing was destroyed. The same records were partitioned along different boundaries.

**Read the fourth column, not the fifth.** A difference is the trade that actually changed family.
The families involved are much larger than that, because most of their records sit in the same
family before and after. An earlier draft of this document reported the fifth column as the amount
at stake, which overstated every event and overstated some of them badly: event 6 reads
CAD 3,788,437,903 by family size and CAD 217,467 by what moved.

## The one reason, stated once

**v1 partitioned by code-number lineage. v2 partitions by what the goods are.** Every one of the
nine is that difference showing up in a particular block. v2 separates two commodities that v1 had
welded together because they shared a code or a residual line, or it joins two lineages that v1 had
kept apart because the code number changed underneath them.

That is the change the rebuild exists to make, and it is why the differences are improvements
rather than regressions. Each event below says which of the two it is and names the evidence.

---

### Event 1. Glands separate from their extracts (IM_0001 + IM_0006, 6 records)

v1 held `3001.10.00.10`, `3001.10.00.90` and `3001.20.00.00` in one family and the heparin line in
another. v2 puts the two `3001.10` glands lines with heparin, and gives `3001.20.00.00` a family of
its own.

**Why.** The `3001.10` lines retired at 2006-12 and their trade went to `3001.90.00.90`, "Heparin
and its salts; human or animal substances for therapeutic or prophylactic uses, nes", which is the
heading's residual. `3001.20.00.00`, extracts of glands, never retired: it trades continuously from
1988-01 to 2026-06. A line that never stopped has no successor and belongs in no other family. v1
grouped the two `3001.10` codes with `3001.20` because the numbers are adjacent. They are not the
same goods and their lineages never meet.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Two records move, carrying CAD 11,343,772.** Both are `3001.10.00.x` glands lines going to the heparin leftover, and the WCO correlation narrowed each to one subheading. Same class as event 8.

### Event 2. Human and animal blood fractions stop sharing a family (21 records)

The five v1 families are IM_0007, IM_0008, IM_0009, IM_0017 and IM_0020. They become five v2
families over the same 21 records. The HS2017 revision replaced the
`3002.10` block with `3002.12`, and v1 followed the code numbers across that boundary while v2
follows the goods.

**Why.** The clearest case is v1's IM_0017. It held `3002.10.29.90` "Blood fractions, nes", which in
2017 becomes `3002.12.00.19` "Human antisera and other human blood fractions, nes". v2 sends that
human line to `IM-3002101022-198801`, with the other human "other uses" lines, and keeps
`3002.10.29.20` "Blood fractions, nes, for use in animals" together with `3002.12.00.29` "Animal
antisera and other animal blood fractions, nes" in `IM-3002102920-198801`.

The distinction being enforced is `origin`, which `decisions/decisive_distinctions.csv` declares as
disqualifying. Human and animal blood fractions are not the same commodity. v1 put them in one
family because the pre-2017 code numbers ran together.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Seven records move, carrying CAD 10,905,807,394, the largest movement in Gate 1.** No analyst override and no weak accept anywhere in it. Every link is settled by the official record or belongs to a line that never retired.

### Event 3. Dried animal blood leaves the microbial residual (IM_0018 + IM_0050 + IM_0051, 19 records)

**Why.** `3002.90.10.40` "Dried blood of animal origin" becomes `3002.90.00.30` in 1998 and trades
to 2026-06 without ever retiring. v1 held it with `3002.90.10.30` "Dried blood of human origin",
which did retire and whose trade went into the `3002.90` residual. v2 keeps the animal line as its
own family, `IM-3002901040-198801`, because it never stopped, and sends the human line into the
residual family with the rest of the microbial preparations.

`3002.49.00.90`, born 2022-01, is also its own family: it is a new HS2022 line and no retiring code
was ruled into it.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Four records move, carrying CAD 10,802,514,370, and the contested part is
one link inside that, not the whole event.** The analyst decided CAD 1,634,256,687 of this event and
almost all of it is a single link: `3002.19.00.00` to `3002.90.00.90`, CAD 1,629,159,701, queue row
213. Its export twin is row 222 in event 9. The rest of the event is routine.

The move those two rows make is not a one-off. A leftover line retires and its goods pass to a
leftover in a different subheading **ten times** in the delivery, carrying CAD 17,745,790,534. The
tariff schedules or the WCO correlation specify or constrain the destination in **six** of them,
which is what makes it a rule rather than a preference. **Two** more carry identical wording and
decide themselves. Rows 213 and 222 are the only two where the record excludes nothing and the
wording is distant, at 0.25.

The structural argument is stronger than the reason originally recorded. `3002.19` was the leftover
for the whole `3002.1` group, not for the immunological sub-block: `3002.13`, `.14` and `.15`
divide immunological products by retail presentation and mixing state and cover that space
completely between them. Goods reached `3002.19` because they matched none of `3002.11` through
`.15`. After 2022 the group keeps no leftover, so they pass to the heading's own leftover.

The trade does not corroborate it and the reason says so. The receiving line gained CAD 268,255,809
in 2022 and two other lines dying that month account for CAD 274,917,486 of it. The alternative,
`3002.15.00.00`, gained CAD 743,047,528 in the same year and is large enough to hide the movement
either way.

*The citation inside row 213's reason was corrected on 2026-08-18. It had named rows 210, 177, 223
and 225 as the same rule. Only 223 is, along with 229. Row 210 moves between two named vaccine
lines, and 177 and 225 are the glands event 223 already covers.*

### Event 4. Toxins follow the toxins (IM_0043 + IM_0047, 9 records)

**Why.** This is the ruling of 2026-08-18, queue rows 66 and 68. `3002.90.00.10` reads "**Toxins**,
toxoids, antitoxins, viruses, bacteriophage and bacterial lysates" and HS2022 split it:
`3002.49.00.20` took "Toxins and similar products" and `3002.90.00.20` took the remaining five
terms. v2 routes the toxin chain, including Ricin and Saxitoxin, to `3002.49.00.20`, and gives
`3002.90.00.20` a family of its own because it names no toxins.

The evidence is the trade series. `3002.90.00.10` ran at 159 to 175 million a year.
`3002.49.00.20` runs at 189, 223 and 269 million in 2023, 2024 and 2025. `3002.90.00.20` runs at 9
to 18 million, under a tenth of the flow. Text similarity favoured the toxoid line at 0.9466
because it kept five of the six words. The trade and the leading term both say otherwise.

**This event moved the crosswalk towards the v1 release rather than away from it.** v1 grouped
Ricin, Saxitoxin and `3002.49.00.20` together, and applying the ruling restored that grouping. The
ruling was argued from the HS2022 structure and the trade series with v1 not consulted.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Four records move, carrying CAD 1,685,442,474.**

### Event 5. Bulk preparations follow the substance, not the alcohol (IM_0061 + IM_0064 + IM_0065, 23 records)

**Why.** v1's IM_0064 held four unrelated bulk lines together because each carried the phrase
"containing more than 23% of absolute ethyl alcohol by volume". v2 sends each to the family of its
substance: `3003.31.91.00` insulin joins the insulin family, `3003.39.91.00` hormones joins the
hormones family, and the remainder stays with the general bulk residual.

**This is not a judgement v2 makes. It is the tariff's own structure.** Statistics Canada placed
insulin in `3003.31`, hormones in `3003.39` and general bulk medicaments in `3003.90`, three
different subheadings, from 1988 onward. The candidate pool offered to `3003.31.91.00` contained
five codes and every one is an insulin line; soya oil emulsions was never among them. The same
holds for the hormone line. v2's partition follows Canada's classification.

**It is v1's grouping that required a judgement, and it crossed the tariff to make it.** The only
thing `3003.31.91.00` and `3003.90.10.00` share is a closing phrase about how much alcohol the
preparation contains, which is a statement about presentation. Insulin and fractioned soya oil
emulsions are not the same goods, and the delivery says so in the code structure itself.

*Corrected 2026-08-18 after review. This section previously read "it is a judgement rather than a
fact the delivery forces", which was wrong: the fact is in the subheadings and was checked against
the candidate pool.*

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Two records move, carrying CAD 140,027**, the smallest movement of the
nine. They are the insulin line and the hormone line that each carried the alcohol phrase, each
returning to the family of its own substance.

### Event 6. Morphine keeps its own line (IM_0137 + IM_0140, 22 records)

**Why.** HS2017 gave morphine its own line, `3004.49.00.10`, separate from the general alkaloid
residual `3004.49.00.90`. v2 follows that: the morphine chain is `IM-3004409050-198801`, and the
rest of the alkaloid block, including cocaine, codeine, theophylline and vincristine, is
`IM-3004409019-198801`. v1 had morphine's later lives in one family and its earlier ones in another.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. One record moves, carrying CAD 217,467.** That is the cocaine line, which
leaves the morphine family for the general alkaloid family.

A larger link inside this event was reviewed at the same time and is sound. `3004.40.00.90`
"Non-narcotic vegetable alkaloids, nes" retires at 2016-12 to `3004.49.00.90` "Medicaments
containing alkaloids or derivatives thereof, nes", carrying CAD 536,767,110 at a wording match of
0.3924. The low score is a style artefact and not a weak link:

  * Canada's own concordance names the destination, recorded as "named by Canada's concordance at
    201701".
  * At the same boundary the record alone sent a sibling, `3004.40.00.30` theophylline, to the very
    same line.
  * Only four candidates existed. Three are ephedrine, pseudoephedrine and norephedrine, named
    substances that a line reading "nes" does not match by definition.
  * Both lines are their block's leftover.

The trade does not track the individual line. The retiring line ran at 31, 71 and 64 million in
2014 to 2016; the receiving line starts at 8.3 million in 2017 and reaches 33.8 million by 2019,
while the morphine line beside it starts at 72.9 million. The block conserves its total. The split
between the two new lines differs from the split before, which is a reclassification inside the
block rather than evidence against the link.

### Event 7. Mixed and unmixed multivitamins separate (IM_0152 + IM_0156, 19 records)

**Why.** "Multivitamins **not** mixed with other supplements" and "multivitamins **mixed** with
other supplements" are different commodities, and Statistics Canada has given them separate codes
throughout. The mixed line ends at `3004.50.00.14`, still trading in 2026-06; the unmixed at
`3004.50.00.13` and `.19`. v2 keeps the two apart and puts the vitamins-iron preparation, which is a
mixture, with the mixed family. The distinction is `mixing`, declared as disqualifying.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. Four records move, carrying CAD 734,339,963.** No analyst override. One accept at a wording match of 0.5200, `3004.50.00.20` to `3004.50.00.19`, carrying CAD 7,548,977 at the 2011-12 renumbering.

### Event 8. Glands and extracts, export side (EX_0001 + EX_0003, 4 records)

**Why.** The same fact as event 1, on the export universe. `3001.10.00` retires 2006-10 into
`3001.90.00`. `3001.20.00` trades to 2026-05 without retiring and is its own family.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18. One record moves, carrying CAD 4,227,953.** That is `3001.10.00`, glands
and other organs, which stopped in October 2006. Its goods went to the heparin line, which is the
leftover for this heading, and it is a member of the ten-link leftover class set out under event 3.
The WCO correlation left one candidate for it. v1 had put glands with `3001.20.00`, extracts, on the
strength of the code numbers being adjacent. Extracts never stopped and is now its own family.

The other three records in this event did not move. Heparin carries CAD 1,898,786,034 between its
two lives and sits in the same family before and after.

**This one is not permanent and the import twin is.** `3001.20.00` stopped one month before the
delivery ends and is resolved `not_a_retirement` on the absence of a termination notice, so it is
one of the 15 rows in `gate1/gate2_tail_disclosure.csv`. If a later delivery declares that line
withdrawn, it becomes a retirement needing a successor and this event changes. The import side,
event 1, does not carry that exposure: `3001.20.00.00` traded in 2026-06, the delivery's own last
month.

### Event 9. The immunological chain reaches the heading residual (EX_0010 + EX_0020, 7 records)

**Why.** This is queue row 222, decided 2026-08-15, and its import twin is row 213. Under HS2022 the
`3002.1` group keeps no residual, so an immunological product that is not unmixed, not mixed and not
in measured doses falls to `3002.90`, the heading's own leftover. `3002.49.00`, born 2022-01, is its
own family. The written decision states outright that the export trade cannot corroborate this and
is not claimed to, and that the import twin carries the evidence.

**CONFIRMED 2026-08-18 together with event 3.** This row carries CAD 6,994,502 and its import twin
carries CAD 1,629,159,701. Both are the same commodity at the same boundary and both are members of
the ten-link class described under event 3, where the official record settles six and identical
wording settles two. The account written for the paper and the reviewers
is in the manuscript materials, not in this package.

## The 19 new export records

`gate1/crosswalk_report.txt` reports 78 v1 export records matched and **19 new in v2**. These are
not a difference to defend. They are the pre-2000 export block that v1's source did not contain: v1
was built from an export extract starting 2000-01 while this delivery starts 1988-01. All 19 begin
between 1988-01 and 1989-01 and every one dies before 2000-01, so none of them creates a family v1
could have had. The plan records this at section 8.

## What is not claimed

No claim is made that v2's partition is right *because* it differs from v1. Each event above rests
on the delivery's own evidence: a line that never retired, a distinction the rules file declares, or
a trade series. Two of the nine, events 4 and 9, rest on analyst rulings whose full reasoning is in
`decisions/stage2_decisions.xlsx` against queue rows 66, 68, 213 and 222.

Event 5 is now among the better-evidenced of the nine rather than the weakest.
